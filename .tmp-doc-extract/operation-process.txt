
This is a manual operation of the system, testing  how  a  real  procurement
officer would and see whether the system forces  the  correct  sequence  and
procurement process.

We test three complete procurements in the same organisation:

   1. Micro procurement — urgent/simple low-value office equipment.

   2. Quotation procurement — laptops/computers.

   3. Tender procurement — university network infrastructure.

This will exercise different branches and prove  that  the  system  supports
multiple procurements at the same time, rather than treating procurement  as
one continuous transaction.
